Independent Quantity Verification — Owner's Representative Capability

On the Montegrande project, the quantity department and the client were at an impasse on 12M m³ of earthwork. I delivered an independent evaluation that unblocked the certification process. That is exactly what an Owner's Representative does on a capital project: verify what the contractor reports, not execute it.

  • Field quantity take-off independent from contractor reporting
  • Payment certification review and validation against field conditions
  • Change order evaluation with alignment log maintenance
  • Experience on both sides — EPC contractor (Odebrecht) and owner/supervision (Andrade Gutiérrez)
Relevant for: Duke Energy, Fluor, and any owner or EPCM firm needing independent QTO and payment cert review on power generation or infrastructure capital projects.

Proprietary Automation That Solves Problems Commercial Software Cannot

ICOD was built in 2 weeks because no commercial tool could process 4,000-point LiDAR cross-sections at tunnel scale. Visual CRP was built because Procore, Aconex, and Citadon do not solve the operational document control problem on fast-track EPC projects — they solve the contractual one. These are not workarounds. They are solutions that external auditors validated alongside the commercial platforms.

  • Excel VBA + AutoCAD API: production-level automation deployed on $500M projects
  • Systems that run alongside Procore/Aconex, not instead of them
  • Ability to build custom tools when standard software hits its limits
  • $1.6M+ in verified savings from document control alone
Relevant for: Any EPC or EPCM environment where project controls teams are managing document workflows in Excel because the enterprise platform does not cover operational needs.
